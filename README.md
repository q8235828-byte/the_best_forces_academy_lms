## 🎖️ About The Platform

**THE BEST FORCES ACADEMY LMS** is Pakistan's premier defense preparatory learning platform engineered for candidates preparing for:
* **Pakistan Army:** PMA Long Course, Technical Cadet Course (TCC), Armed Forces Nursing Services (AFNS), Army Medical Cadet (AMC), Soldier & Clerk batches.
* **Pakistan Navy:** PN Cadet Commission, Sailor, Naval Police, FMT, Marine, and Technical branches.
* **Pakistan Air Force (PAF):** General Duty Pilot (GDP), College of Aeronautical Engineering (CAE), Air Defence (AD), Aerotrade, and Admin branches.
* **Cadet & Military Colleges:** Entry test preparation for Hassan Abdal, MCJ, MCM, MCS, PAF Sargodha, Petaro, and provincial cadet institutions.
* **Entry Tests & Academics:** MDCAT, ECAT, LAT, N-CAT, Universities NET/FAST, and Spoken English.

## 🚀 Key Architectural Features

1. **Free Demo Lecture 1 Rule:**
   - Every course's Lecture 1 (`order_index = 1`) is open and free to all visitors.
   - Lessons 2+ enforce an automated payment verification paywall.
2. **Anti-Piracy Floating Watermark:**
   - Real-time floating watermark displaying candidate name & phone number shifting across coordinates every 15 seconds to deter unauthorized recording.
3. **Computerized Intelligence & Academic Engine:**
   - GHQ/NHQ/Air HQ standardized computerized exam terminal with countdown timer, question palette, and detailed explanations.
4. **ISSB 10-Second WAT (Word Association Test) Simulator:**
   - Authentic 20-word psychological battery with automated 10-second timer transitions.
5. **National Candidate Leaderboard (`/portal/leaderboard`):**
   - Live podium rankings (Gold, Silver, Bronze) sorted by score and completion speed.
6. **Smart ISSB Admission Form + OCR Auto-Fill:**
   - Upload CNIC/B-Form to extract biodata and generate an official printable A4 Roll Number Slip with verification seal.
7. **Admin Control Suite:**
   - Dynamic Course Fee Manager (`/admin/courses`) and Master Student Registry (`/admin/students`).
8. **Cloudflare D1 & R2 Native:**
   - Edge database queries (SQLite) and high-speed R2 object storage for video streaming and course materials.
